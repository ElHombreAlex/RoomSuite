# Cliproom third-party components

MotionRoom GIF export uses gifenc 1.0.3, distributed under the MIT license. Its license text is included in `mcp/node_modules/gifenc/LICENSE.md`.

The optional MCP dependencies include Mediabunny and `@mediabunny/aac-encoder` 1.64.0, distributed under MPL-2.0; their package directories include their license texts. The AAC extension contains a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg's AAC encoder. FFmpeg is available under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later; a copy is provided in `licenses/FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt`. The extension's source and build instructions are available at https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny/tree/v1.64.0/packages/aac-encoder, and FFmpeg source is available at https://ffmpeg.org/download.html.
