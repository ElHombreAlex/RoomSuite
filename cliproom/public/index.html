Transcribe a clip locally

The optional speech engine downloads about 63 MiB to your server. Speech is processed on this device, with no audio sent to the download hosts. It uses this clip’s source without timeline volume, fades or other tracks. Use a source up to 2 minutes and 32 MiB, and a selected range up to 30 seconds. Review all words and timing.
