THE DETAILS

Fine tune.

Changes are applied when you play or export.

The default leaves approximately 3 dB of headroom before EQ and compression.

Measures processed mono/stereo audio up to 60 seconds.

Equalizer & dynamics

Adjust three broad frequency bands; enable compression to reduce the difference between quiet and loud sounds.

Compression uses a 3 ms attack and 250 ms release.

A local noise gate fades down quiet sections; it cannot remove noise underneath loud speech or music. Mono/stereo only, up to 6 million channel samples.

The ceiling clips samples after gain and fades; strong limiting can distort sound. It does not measure inter-sample true peaks.

Take it with you

Open in Cliproom ↗

Sending uploads a processed WAV and an editable source with its settings. Cliproom uses the processed copy; reopen the source in SoundRoom to change the processing.

Save a .soundroom session to keep editable settings, or export a WAV to share the finished sound.
